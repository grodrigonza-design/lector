Las imágenes de varias viñetas NO van a Flow: se animan en CapCut con fotogramas clave (ahorra créditos).
Total: **10 clips de Flow ≈ 100 créditos** (2 días de créditos gratis).

## Prompts de Flow
**Clip 1 — Adjuntar: imagen 1**
```
Slow push-in down the aisle of the farmers' market. The blue tarps sway gently in the breeze, leaf shadows flicker on the ground, and people in the background walk and shop. Peaceful afternoon.
```
**Clip 2 — Adjuntar: imagen 2**
```
The camera slowly tracks backward as the long-haired man in the black trench coat walks calmly toward it with a faint crooked smile, never blinking. The red, violet and black shadow under his feet crawls and pulses across the ground like living ink, and the tarps bend toward him. The vendor keeps shouting and gesturing, the little girl sobs holding her stuffed bunny. In the background, the hooded grey-bearded man follows him with clenched fists.
```
**Clip 3 — Adjuntar: imagen 4**
```
Static wide shot of the quiet street. A soft breeze moves the leaves of the big tree, the sun-dappled shadows shift slowly on the asphalt, and the clouds drift over the green mountains. Calm, ordinary afternoon.
```
**Clip 4 — Adjuntar: imagen 5**
```
The father walks across the living room carrying the baby bottle, smiling tiredly, without looking down. The mother gently adjusts the yellow blanket over the baby in the crib. The ceiling fan turns slowly. The boy sitting on the floor follows his father with his eyes, then lowers his gaze to the ball in his arms. Slow push-in toward the boy.
```
**Clip 5 — Adjuntar: imagen 7**
```
Slow motion, high angle. The boy runs after the ball into the street while the dark car comes slowly out of the curve with its headlights on. The thin black-violet shadow joining the boy's shadow to the man under the tree stretches and pulses like a living thread. The man in the trench coat barely moves, watching calmly. The shot ends before the car reaches the boy.
```
**Clip 6 — Adjuntar: imagen 8**
```
Extreme slow motion. The boy reaches for the ball, his hair moving in the wind, as the car headlights grow brighter and brighter until the light fills the whole frame and fades to white. No impact is shown.
```
**Clip 7 — Adjuntar: imagen 9**
```
Total stillness. A few leaves fall slowly onto the street. The boy lies on the asphalt with his eyes open, his chest moving only faintly. Under the big tree, the man in the trench coat smiles slightly. Very slow push-in toward the boy.
```
**Clip 8 — Adjuntar: imagen 10**
```
Time is frozen: leaves hang motionless in the air. The winged grey-bearded man keeps one hand on the boy's chest and the other on his head; a soft pale light flows from his hand into the boy. His ash-grey wings tremble and slowly grow dimmer, and a little ash drifts from their tips. His breathing is heavy. In the background, the man in the trench coat watches from under the tree.
```
**Clip 9 — Adjuntar: imagen 12**
```
The soft light under the grey-winged man's hand fades. His wings fold slowly and he dissolves into ash and shadow, disappearing, leaving the boy lying alone on the street. The frozen leaves start falling again.
```
**Clip 10 — Adjuntar: imagen 15**
```
The father kneels on the street with one hand on the boy's head and the other on his chest, his arm stretched out, keeping a little distance. He closes his eyes and lets out a long breath of relief, tears on his cheeks. The boy slowly turns his eyes away from his father toward the house, where his mother stands in the doorway holding the baby, her hand over her mouth.
```
