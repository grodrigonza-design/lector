# Capítulo 1, Parte 1 «La pelota» — Prompts de video para Flow (versión final de estilo)

Cómo usar:
- En Flow, modo **Ingredientes a video**, formato **9:16**. Subir las hojas indicadas en cada plano (máximo 3 por plano; si Flow deja menos, priorizar las primeras).
- Copiar el **bloque de estilo** al inicio de cada prompt.
- Generar 2–3 versiones por plano. Silenciar el audio de Flow en CapCut (voz y música van aparte).
- Narración y diálogos: ver `01-parte-1-la-pelota.md`.

**Bloque de estilo (pegar siempre al inicio):**
```
Vertical 9:16. Hand-painted 2D animation: full-color gouache backgrounds with visible brushstrokes, warm tropical afternoon light, small town in Costa Rica. Characters drawn semi-realistic anime-comic style with bold black ink lines and soft shading, keeping their natural colors. No text, no subtitles, no music, no voices, ambient sound only.
```

## 1 — El gancho (0–6 s)
```
A white and red soccer ball rolls slowly by itself across the warm asphalt of the quiet residential street, past the black iron gate of the sand-colored house, under the big shade tree. A single worn ash-grey feather drifts down from the sky, drawn in rough charcoal strokes, shedding a little ash. Low camera at ground level, slow motion, melancholic stillness.
```

## 2 — El mercado (6–12 s)
```
A busy small-town farmers' market in Costa Rica: fruit stalls, plastic tarps, people shopping. Azael walks calmly through the crowd, one hand in his coat pocket, a faint crooked smile, never blinking; his figure has deep black and purple shading with halftone dots and ink edges that slightly dissolve. As he passes, the people closest to him lose their color and turn grey: a fruit vendor starts arguing with his wife and a little girl suddenly bursts into tears. In the background, the tall heavy grey-bearded man in a worn hooded jacket follows him, his outline drawn with charcoal strokes, and clenches his fists. Slow tracking shot.
```

## 3 — La casa (12–18 s)
```
Inside the cozy middle-class living room: ceramic floor, brown fabric sofa, flat TV, family photos, ceiling fan, warm light through the window. The tired mother in a red blouse leans over a wooden crib with a newborn in a pale yellow blanket. The father with thick dark glasses and a mustache walks past carrying a baby bottle, tired but smiling. On the floor, the 6-year-old boy with straight light-blond hair, light-blue polo shirt and soft watercolor edges on his clothes, holds his white and red soccer ball and looks up at them, waiting. Nobody looks at him.
```

## 4 — El corredor (18–24 s)
```
The 6-year-old boy with straight light-blond hair and light-blue polo shirt walks out alone onto the front porch of the sand-colored house, holding his ball; the watercolor tones of his figure turn slightly cold and grey. Across the street, in the shade of the big old tree, Azael stands still with his hands in his pockets, staring at the boy without blinking, his black-purple ink shading dissolving at the edges. Wide shot, ominous stillness, leaves barely moving.
```

## 5 — Claukia (24–30 s)
```
Close-up: a tiny hummingbird made of soft warm white-gold light, small body, fast blurred wings, faint cyan and magenta edges, hovers perfectly still in front of the face of the grey-bearded man in the worn hooded jacket, drawn with charcoal-stroke shadows. He looks at it with tired eyes and whispers softly, like greeting an old friend. The hummingbird turns and darts away down the street, leaving a faint trail of light.
```

## 6 — La pelota (30–36 s)
```
The white and red ball bounces off the black iron gate and rolls into the street. The little blond boy runs after it without looking. From the curve at the end of the street, bright headlights appear and tires screech. Cut to black before any impact.
```
(Si Flow lo rechaza: quitar el carro y dejar solo «bright headlights and screeching tires, cut to black».)

## 7 — Silencio (36–42 s)
```
Quiet aftermath on the sunny street: the white and red ball keeps rolling alone and stops at the curb. The little blond boy lies still on the asphalt, eyes open, peaceful, no blood, no visible injuries; the watercolor colors of his figure slowly wash out toward white. Across the street, under the big tree, Azael gives a faint cold smile. Slow push-in.
```

## 8 — El tiempo se detiene (42–48 s)
```
Time freezes: leaves hang motionless in the air above the street. The tall, heavy grey-bearded man in an olive work shirt kneels beside the little blond boy; huge worn ash-grey wings with broken feathers open behind him, a faint soft cold glow along their edges, no sparks. His shadows are drawn with charcoal strokes and a little ash drifts from the wing tips. He places his large scarred hand on the boy's chest and a faint pale light passes into the boy; his wings dim and grow greyer with the effort, his breathing heavy, eyes wet with very old sadness and tenderness.
```

## 9 — Azael habla (48–54 s)
```
Across the frozen street, Azael stands under the big tree, stops smiling and says one short sentence with a cold expression (silent, no voice audio). The kneeling grey-winged man slowly raises his head and holds his gaze calmly, without anger and without fear, for a long moment, then lowers his eyes back to the boy and keeps giving him light. Shot-reverse-shot, tense silence.
```

## 10 — Despierta (54–60 s, + 2 s de tarjeta en CapCut)
```
The little blond boy gasps for air and opens his eyes. Above him, his father with thick dark glasses kneels on the asphalt, crying and shouting. In the background, at the door of the sand-colored house, the mother in a red blouse stands holding the newborn wrapped in a pale yellow blanket. Boy's point of view: focus shifts from the father to the mother and the baby. The boy's watercolor tones return, but cold and grey-blue.
```
